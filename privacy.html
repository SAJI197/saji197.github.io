Privacy policy — BAB AL FALAH Backups

Effective 30 October 2026.

BAB AL FALAH Backups is a private, internal backup utility operated by BAB AL FALAH for its own records. Its only user is BAB AL FALAH.

Google user data the app accesses

  • The app requests one Google permission: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file. With it, the app can create, read and check only the files it has created itself in BAB AL FALAH's Google Drive. It cannot see, open or change any other file.
  • It does not request access to email, contacts, calendar, profile details beyond what Google requires for sign-in, or any other Google service.

How the data is used

  • The app uploads encrypted backup files of BAB AL FALAH's own accounting system, reads them back to confirm they arrived intact, and downloads them when a backup is restored or tested. It uses Google user data for nothing else.
  • Every file is encrypted with AES-256-GCM on BAB AL FALAH's own computer before it is uploaded. The encryption key is never sent to Google or to anyone else.

Sharing, selling and advertising

  • No data is sold, shared with or transferred to any third party.
  • No data is used for advertising, analytics, profiling or training of any kind.
  • The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Storage and retention

  • The backup files stay in BAB AL FALAH's own Google Drive until BAB AL FALAH decides to remove them. The app never deletes or overwrites a backup.
  • The sign-in credentials the app needs are kept encrypted on BAB AL FALAH's own computer.

Removing access

Access can be withdrawn at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Withdrawing access does not delete any backup file already in the Drive.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the support email shown on the app's Google sign-in screen.
